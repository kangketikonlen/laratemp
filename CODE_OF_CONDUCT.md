# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project aims to be respectful, practical, and welcoming.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect
- assume good intent while giving honest feedback
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people
- be patient with questions and different experience levels
- help keep discussions constructive and relevant to the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, abuse, or intimidation
- discriminatory or hateful language
- personal attacks or insults
- deliberate disruption, trolling, or bad-faith engagement
- sharing private or sensitive information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness harmful behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers when possible.

## Collaboration Reminder

Clear disagreement is fine. Disrespect is not.
